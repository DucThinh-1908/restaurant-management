Who we are

Honouring the familiar, discovering the new

At GoD Fine Dining, our philosophy begins with respect for Vietnamese culinary heritage. We take inspiration from regional dishes, family traditions and the ingredients that make our food culture so distinctive.

Our approach is contemporary but never forgets its roots: thoughtful preparation, balanced flavours and genuine hospitality at every table.

Located in Da Nang, GoD is imagined as a place to celebrate both everyday moments and special occasions.
